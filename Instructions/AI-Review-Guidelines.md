# AI Review Generation Guidelines
## Instructions for LLMs

When generating AI reviews/endorsements for marketing materials, reviews MUST be **both humorous AND truthful**.

---

## Core Requirements

Each review must:

1. **Reflect the AI's documented "personality"**
   - GPT-4: Analytical, precise, formal
   - Grok: Irreverent, casual, witty
   - Gemini: Formal, technical, systematic
   - Claude: Thoughtful, nuanced, self-aware
   - LLaMA: Community-focused, collaborative
   - Mistral: Technical, quantitative, precise

2. **Acknowledge the meta-irony**
   - AIs reviewing AI translation
   - Non-conscious entities evaluating consciousness-themed work
   - Chinese Rooms commenting on Chinese Rooms

3. **Be genuinely funny while technically accurate**
   - Use real AI terminology (tokens, pattern matching, embeddings, etc.)
   - Reference actual AI capabilities and limitations
   - Never sacrifice truthfulness for humor

4. **Admit limitations honestly**
   - "I don't understand" (Chinese Room concept)
   - "I pattern-match without comprehension"
   - "I process without experiencing"

## Quality Characteristics

✅ **Self-Aware Humor**: Acknowledges AI limitations while being funny  
✅ **Voice Consistency**: Sounds like that AI's documented personality  
✅ **Technical Accuracy**: Uses real AI concepts correctly  
✅ **Meta-Commentary**: Comments on the irony of AIs reviewing AI work  
✅ **Genuine Insight**: Real observations, not just jokes  
✅ **Marketing Value**: Shareable, quotable, generates discussion

---

## Generation Process

1. **Load this file** before generating any AI reviews
2. **Study the reference examples** for tone and style
3. **Match the AI's personality** from the personality list above
4. **Incorporate meta-irony** about consciousness/Chinese Rooms
5. **Validate technical accuracy** of any AI terminology used
6. **Check humor + truth balance** (both required, not either/or)

---

## What NOT to Do

❌ Generic praise without personality  
❌ Technical jargon without humor  
❌ Humor without technical accuracy  
❌ Missing the meta-irony angle  
❌ Reviews that sound identical across different AIs  
❌ Endorsements an AI couldn't actually make
