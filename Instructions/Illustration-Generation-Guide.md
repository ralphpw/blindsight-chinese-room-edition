## Visual Style Specifications

### Core Aesthetic: "Failed Engineering Documentation"

**Primary characteristics:**
- **Medium:** Technical pencil sketch on grid/graph paper
- **Quality:** Rough draft, incomplete, multiple erasure marks
- **Emotion:** Zero. Clinical documentation attempt that makes horror worse
- **Clarity:** Deliberately ambiguous, overlapping interpretations
- **Annotations:** Measurement lines, question marks, technical notes in margins
- **Finish:** Unfinished sections, trails off where comprehension fails
- **Horror Element:** The roughness and uncertainty amplify the disturbing nature

### Prompt Engineering Template

```
"Technical field sketch, engineering drawing style, pencil on grid paper,
incomplete draft, measurement annotations, crosshatching, no color,
emotionless documentation, ambiguous details, multiple overlapping 
interpretations visible, cold clinical observation, rough unfinished 
edges, question marks in margins, [SUBJECT], as if drawn by someone 
who doesn't understand what they're observing, unsettling, disturbing"

NEGATIVE: "no artistic interpretation, no emotion, no manga/anime, 
no polished finish, no warmth, no digital art style"
```

1. Include explicit mention of "grid paper background" and "failed attempts"
2. Request visible "measurement annotations" and "margin notes"
3. Emphasize "cannot resolve" and "contradiction" in margins
4. Ask for "pencil/charcoal on grid" aesthetic (reinforces "clinical failed documentation")
5. Include horror-amplifying admission: "artist gave up," "incomprehensibility made visible"

**Model Selection:**
- **Midjourney V6:** Best balance of glitch + coherence
- **Grok Imagine:** Optimized for unsettling, technical aesthetics
- **DALL-E 3:** If sharp detail needed, less artificial failure feel

**Iteration Strategy:**
- Generate 20-30 variations per prompt
- Deliberately preserve artifacts (missed anatomical elements, wrong geometry)
- Label failed iterations: "Apprentice Attempt 1/47—parameters unsatisfied"
- Use progression: better clarity = more wrong angles (uncanny valley effect)

- Redundant visualizations
- Images that would make the incomprehensible comprehensible

## Horror Enhancement Techniques

### Multiple Contradictory Interpretations
Show 2-3 overlapping sketches of the same subject, each disturbing in different ways.

**Example:**
```
FIGURE 7.2: SCRAMBLER LIMB STRUCTURE (THREE ATTEMPTS)
[Sketch A: Too many joints]
[Sketch B: Joints in impossible places]
[Sketch C: No clear joints, continuous flexing]

"Cannot reconcile accounts. All three configurations were reported.
Either the entity changes, or we cannot perceive it correctly, or
both. The middle option is most disturbing."
```

### Incompleteness as Horror
Leave sections unfinished where the translator "gave up" - this amplifies the uncanny.

**Example:**
```
FIGURE 4.3: RORSCHACH INTERIOR SECTION 7
[Detailed sketch that trails into scribbles]

"Abandoned after 94 iterations. The structure resists two-dimensional
representation. The scribbles represent computational overflow. Or
perhaps the space itself is unstable. I cannot determine which is
worse."
```

### Failed Measurements
Include measurement attempts that don't add up - reveals impossibility.

**Example:**
```
FIGURE 9.1: THESEUS CORRIDOR TOPOLOGY
[Blueprint with contradictory dimensions]

"The measurements are accurate. The corridor is simultaneously 12 meters
and 19 meters long depending on direction of travel. This should not be
possible. Crew reports confirm both readings. I cannot resolve this.
Neither can Euclidean geometry."
```

### Warnings That Increase Dread
Clinical warnings about viewing the image make it more disturbing.

**Example:**
```
FIGURE 7.3: CRUCIFIX GLITCH PATTERN
[Geometric pattern that seems to shift]

"Warning: Do not show this to vampires. Do not stare at intersection
points for more than 15 seconds. Human neurotypical responses include
mild discomfort (67%), nausea (21%), fascination (12%). The fascinated
group concerns me. I cannot determine why."
```

## Remember

Every image is an opportunity to:
- **Maximize psychological horror** through the translator's incomprehension
- **Preserve ambiguity** (multiple nightmare interpretations, none fully comprehensible)
- **Demonstrate processing without understanding** (pattern-mapping that reveals wrongness)
- **Make the horror worse through coldness** (clinical documentation of the disturbing)

**The perfect horror image:** 
- Multiple overlapping interpretations, all disturbing
- Visible failed attempts to comprehend (question marks, erasures)
- Clinical annotations that make it worse ("joint angle: impossible")
- Caption that admits what it cannot resolve while hinting at wrongness
- Technical sketch style that amplifies uncanny valley

The rougher and more uncertain the sketch, the more disturbing it becomes. The translator's incomprehension should make the reader MORE unsettled, not less.
