# Literary Style Analysis Template

**Purpose:** This template defines all literary style elements that must be analyzed from Peter Watts' *Blindsight* to ensure authentic translation voice.

**Usage:** An LLM (Opus 4.1) will read the original novel and fill in every section with specific examples, patterns, and rules. The completed analysis becomes required reading for all translation tasks.

---

## 1. Narrative Voice & POV

### First-Person Limitations
- [ ] How does Keeton's synthesist perspective shape narration?
- [ ] What insights does he provide that feel analytical vs. emotional?
- [ ] How does his lack of affect influence tone?
- [ ] Examples of clinical observation vs. human warmth

### Unreliable Narrator Elements
- [ ] Where does Keeton misinterpret events?
- [ ] How does his disability color his understanding?
- [ ] What does he miss that readers should catch?
- [ ] Examples of self-deception or blind spots

### Temporal Shifts
- [ ] How are flashbacks introduced and structured?
- [ ] What verb tenses mark present vs. past?
- [ ] How does memory/recall feel different from real-time?
- [ ] Transition patterns between timelines

## Completion Checklist

When filling out this template, ensure:

- [ ] Every section has specific examples from the text (with chapter references)
- [ ] Patterns are described with enough detail to replicate
- [ ] Rules are actionable (not just observations)
- [ ] Examples are representative of typical usage
- [ ] Exceptions and variations are noted
- [ ] Context is provided (why this technique works here)
- [ ] Comparison to norms (how Watts differs from typical prose)
- [ ] Translation implications (what to preserve vs. adapt)

## Notes for Analyzer

**Your job is to:**
1. Read *Blindsight* with this template open
2. Fill in every [ ] checkbox with detailed analysis
3. Provide 3-5 examples for each element
4. Note chapter/page references for examples
5. Identify patterns (not just individual instances)
6. Explain WHY each technique works
7. Suggest how to replicate in translation

**This is NOT:**
- A book review or literary criticism
- An academic essay
- A summary of the plot
- Personal opinions about quality

**This IS:**
- A technical manual for replicating Watts' style
- A reference guide for translators
- A pattern library of prose techniques
- A rulebook for maintaining voice consistency

**Quality Standard:**
If an LLM reads your completed analysis, they should be able to write new prose that SOUNDS like Peter Watts, even without reading the original novel. That's the bar.
