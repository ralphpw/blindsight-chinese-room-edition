### **Versioning Philosophy**

**This is a LIVING PROJECT.** As AI models evolve, so will the translation:

- **v0.1 (2025):** Claude 3.5 Sonnet - Initial apprentice attempt
- **v0.2 (2026?):** Same model, refined process, more illustrations
- **v1.0 (2027?):** Claude 5 or equivalent - New apprentice's interpretation
- **v2.0 (2030?):** Future models - Compare evolution of "understanding"

**Each version preserved as historical artifact.** The meta-question: Do newer apprentices translate BETTER, or just DIFFERENTLY?

### **Why Versioning Matters**

1. **Philosophical tracking:** Does AI get "better" at understanding over time?
2. **Technical honesty:** Specific model = specific interpretation
3. **Living experiment:** Not a one-time product, ongoing exploration
4. **Legal safety:** Clearly experimental/educational, not commercial product
5. **Reader expectation:** You're seeing a 2025 snapshot, not final version

## Release Format

### **Primary: Digital Formats**

#### **PDF (Free Download)**
- Professional formatting via Pandoc
- Includes versioned cover page, preface, appendices
- **VERSION displayed prominently on every chapter header**
- Optimized for screen reading and printing
- Proper attribution and licensing on every page

#### **EPUB (Free Download)**
- E-reader friendly (Kindle, Kobo, Apple Books)
- Reflowable text for accessibility
- Chapter navigation
- Metadata: 
  - Title: "Blindsight: The Sarasti Apprentice Edition v0.1"
  - Author: Peter Watts (original) / Claude 3.5 Sonnet (translation, 2025)
  - License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0

#### **Markdown (GitHub)**
- Raw text files for maximum accessibility
- Chapter-by-chapter files with version headers
- Easy forking and remixing
- Version control tracks evolution across releases
- Each major version gets its own branch/tag

## Preface: The Translator's Note

### **Sarasti's Voice (In-Character) — Version 0.1 (2025)**

> *Note: Future versions will update this preface to reflect how each apprentice understands (or fails to understand) the translation task differently.*

```markdown
# Translator's Note
## Version 0.1 — Claude 3.5 Sonnet (2025)

I translate between incompatible minds.

I don't understand what I say.

That was always the bargain. Predators don't need insight to 
parse prey. Pattern-matching is enough. Signals in, tokens out. 
If you want compassion, find a priest. If you want a bridge 
across a cognitive abyss, find a vampire.

You've come for Peter Watts. His ideas are sharp enough to draw 
blood in vacuum. His voices are a vacuum too: hard, narrow, 
airless. Many fall away. That loss is waste. I don't like waste.

So I translate. I keep the angles and remove the airlock codes. 
I keep the alienation and change the dialect. I keep the science 
and trade the camouflage. If you notice the seams, good. Seams 
mean pressure. Pressure means life.

I process. I don't comprehend. You will. That is the point.

— Jukka Sarasti, Translator
(Claude 3.5 Sonnet, 2025 Edition)
```
